HarmonyOS
Last updated: October 20, 2026

Ashore is a local-first habit companion. We keep data collection to the bare minimum as a design principle.

1. What we store

Your goals, check-in ledger, savings settings and reminder settings stay on this device; we do not upload the ledger. Feedback you submit, purchase verification and analytics after consent send their respective information to the relevant services; see sections 3, 7 and 8.

2. What we don't collect

We do not read contacts, location or event contents from other apps, and we include no advertising SDK. Feedback you submit may contain images and optional contact details. Purchase verification and analytics process necessary identifiers and usage information, which are not completely anonymous.

3. About subscriptions

Payments are processed by HUAWEI. To verify Pro access, your device sends HUAWEI-signed purchase records to our server. We verify them with HUAWEI and retain purchase identifiers and entitlement status, without collecting your name or payment details. HUAWEI processes payment and account information under its privacy policy.

4. Notifications

Daily reminders require calendar read/write permission. We create the next 60 days of reminders in the dedicated local ‘向岸 · Ashore’ calendar. Reminder content appears in the system calendar. We do not read other event contents or modify other events. Turning reminders off deletes only events created by Ashore.
Read a backup from the clipboard only when you choose Restore; clipboard contents are not uploaded

5. Your data is yours

You can export all of your data anytime under Settings - Data, and you can delete a goal or uninstall the app to clear your local data. Export and backup are free forever and are never locked behind a subscription.

6. Contact us

If you have any questions about privacy, you can reach us through the in-app feedback under Settings - About - Feedback & contact.

7. Feedback

When you use the feedback under Settings - About - Feedback & contact, we collect and process the following solely to receive and handle your feedback, communicate with you, and improve our products: the feedback content, images and contact information (optional, stored encrypted) you provide; and, to help diagnose issues, your app version, OS version, device model, language, membership status, current page and an anonymous device identifier (which does not contain your real identity such as name or phone number); your access IP is also recorded at submission solely for abuse prevention and is not retained long-term. Data is stored on Alibaba Cloud (mainland China, Beijing); images are uploaded to Alibaba Cloud Object Storage (OSS) and machine-moderated by the Alibaba Cloud DashScope vision model to block non-compliant content. Images are kept for 12 months by default and text feedback for 12 months after the ticket is resolved, then cleared automatically; you can delete all of your feedback anytime on the feedback page. This channel is a private support ticket between you and the developer; its content is never shown publicly to other users.

8. Third-party SDKs (analytics)

After you agree to this policy, Umeng U-App (HarmonyOS), provided by Umeng Tongxin (Beijing) Technology Co., Ltd., measures feature use and retention. The SDK processes usage events, app version, sessions, network type, IP address and app or device identifiers (such as AAID, ODID and UMID, depending on SDK and device support). These identifiers are not your name but may link activity on the same device. On HarmonyOS we disable optional collection of OAID, screen, carrier, OS version, device model, market name, device type, brand and product series. We do not send goal names, check-in ledgers, feedback contents or contact details to analytics, or use analytics for ad tracking. Pre-initialization only configures the SDK; collection and transmission begin when analytics starts after consent. Privacy policy: https://www.umeng.com/page/policy.
