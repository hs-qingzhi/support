3. Information Processing for the Feedback Feature

When you use "Feedback" within the App, we collect and process the following information solely to receive and handle your feedback, communicate with you, and improve our products:

  • 3.1 Information you provide: Feedback content, images, and contact information (optional). Contact information is stored encrypted.
  • 3.2 Information attached automatically to help diagnose issues: App version, OS version, device model, language, membership status, and current page, along with a device identifier (which does not contain your real identity such as name or phone number); your access IP is also recorded at submission solely for abuse prevention and security, and is kept with the server access logs (see Section 7.2).
  • 3.3 Storage and third parties: Data is stored on Alibaba Cloud (within mainland China, Beijing). Images are uploaded to Alibaba Cloud Object Storage (OSS) and machine-moderated by an Alibaba Cloud Model Studio vision model to block non-compliant content; this image moderation is an entrusted-processing arrangement with Alibaba Cloud.
  • 3.4 Retention: Images are deleted automatically after 12 months by default; text feedback is retained for 12 months after the ticket is resolved, then cleared automatically.
  • 3.5 Your rights: You can delete all of your feedback (including images) within "My Feedback"; records are removed immediately upon request and images are cleared thereafter.
  • 3.6 Non-public: This channel is a private support ticket between you and us; its content is never shown publicly to other users.

4. Cloud Recognition for Smart Capture

"Cloud Recognition" is off by default. The first time cloud recognition is needed, the App explains what is uploaded and who processes it, and lets you choose "Allow Cloud Recognition" or "On-Device Only"; until you choose, or if you choose on-device only, no image is uploaded. Once you allow it, adding an item with Smart Capture (as a member or within the free preview period) or tapping "Re-identify with AI" on the item detail page uploads an image for cloud recognition to identify the item's category, color, season, material and style and to generate a name. We process the following information:

  • 4.1 On-device processing: Background removal, initial classification and color extraction are performed by your phone's system capabilities and are not uploaded. Your item photos (including cutouts and original photos) are stored only locally on your phone.
  • 4.2 Information uploaded: The App compresses the item image you selected (longest side no more than 768 pixels; when the background can be removed, only the background-removed image is uploaded) and uploads it to our server, together with the App's interface language and the device identifier generated by the system for this App (IDFV, which contains no name or phone number). If a person is wearing the item in the photo, the uploaded image may include that person; please photograph the item on its own where possible. We do not perform facial recognition.
  • 4.3 Processors and location: Our server is hosted on Alibaba Cloud (within mainland China, Beijing). After receiving the image, our server passes it to Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Qwen vision model, Beijing region) for recognition and returns only the recognition result to the App. This recognition is an entrusted-processing arrangement with Alibaba Cloud, and we require it to process the image only to complete that recognition.
  • 4.4 Image retention: Our own server does not store the images you upload and does not use them for any purpose other than recognition; recognition results are stored only locally on your phone. Alibaba Cloud Model Studio may retain call data under its service agreement and privacy policy while providing the service (for example for security auditing and content safety) and states that it does not use such data for model training. If you would like it deleted, contact us as described in Section 8.6 and we will help submit the request to Alibaba Cloud.
  • 4.5 Abuse prevention: To limit daily usage and prevent abuse of the service, we count daily requests per device identifier and per access IP; these counts are kept for at most 2 days and then deleted automatically. Each device can currently use cloud recognition up to 50 times a day; beyond that, on-device recognition is used automatically for the rest of the day and Smart Capture keeps working.
  • 4.6 Your choice: You can turn "Cloud Recognition" on or off at any time in "Profile > Privacy Settings". With it off, Smart Capture uses on-device recognition only and no images are uploaded, and "Re-identify with AI" is unavailable; when the network is unavailable or cloud recognition fails, Smart Capture likewise uses on-device recognition only. Requests already sent before you turn it off will be completed.

  • 5.2 Local reminders: Daily outfit reminders, monthly report reminders and membership renewal reminders are generated locally on your phone and do not upload personal information; notification reminders work only if you allow notifications. For the in-app renewal reminder, see Section 3.2 of the Paid Membership Agreement.
  • 5.3 Backup: "Backup Closet" packages your wardrobe data and photos into a file, and you choose where to save or share it. We do not receive that file.
  • 5.4 Widget: The home screen widget only reads wardrobe data stored on your phone and does not upload anything.

7. Storage and Protection

  • 7.1 Local storage: Your wardrobe data (items, photos, outfits, wear records, etc.) is stored only locally on your phone and we cannot access it. Uninstalling the App deletes this data within the App, so please use "Backup Closet" to save a copy before uninstalling or changing phones; backup files you export are not deleted when you uninstall and you need to delete them yourself. In addition, to restore your membership status and feedback history after reinstalling, a small amount of information (membership expiry date and the device credential used for Feedback) is stored in the system Keychain and may remain after uninstalling.
  • 7.2 Server storage location and retention: The small amount of information we process (feedback, Cloud Recognition usage counts, server access logs) is stored within mainland China (Beijing). Server access logs (including IP address, access time and request path, but not the images or feedback you submit) are used for security operations and, in line with the Cybersecurity Law's requirement to retain network logs for no less than six months, are kept for 6 months and then deleted automatically; during that period they are used only for security operations and lawful investigations, with strictly limited access.
  • 7.3 Cross-border transfer: Our server and the Alibaba Cloud services we use are located within mainland China (Beijing). If you use the App outside mainland China, information sent to our server, such as feedback and cloud recognition requests, is transferred to mainland China for processing; where third-party SDKs and Apple's push service process information is as described by each provider.
  • 7.4 Security measures: We use HTTPS encryption, request signature verification, rate limiting and other measures to protect your information. However, no security measure is completely secure. If a personal information security incident occurs, we will notify you and report to the authorities as required by law.

8. Your Rights

  • 8.1 Access and copy: Your wardrobe data is on your phone; you can view it in the App at any time and export a copy via "Profile > Backup Closet".
  • 8.2 Correction: You can edit an item's name, category, color and other details on the item detail page at any time.
  • 8.3 Deletion: You can delete any item, outfit or inspiration, or uninstall the App to delete data within the App (see Section 7.1 for the small amount of information in the Keychain and for backups you saved); feedback can be deleted in "My Feedback". Images uploaded for Cloud Recognition are not stored on our server; for call data retained by Alibaba Cloud Model Studio, see Section 4.4.

With "Analytics & Ads" off, the App no longer initializes the analytics, push and ad SDKs (takes effect after restarting the App). After you withdraw consent, we stop the related new processing and the related features stop working or become non-personalized, while wardrobe management and other core features keep working; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it.
  • 8.5 Account deletion: The App has no accounts, so there is nothing to close; uninstalling the App deletes data within the App (see Section 7.1 for the Keychain and backups you saved).
  • 8.6 Exercising your rights: If the above does not meet your needs, or you would like us to delete information about you held on our servers, contact us at 954964571@qq.com. We will handle and reply within 15 business days.

9. Protection of Minors

The App is intended mainly for adults and is not directed to children under 14; we do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you are under 14, please read this policy with your guardian and use features that send information to us (such as Cloud Recognition and Feedback) only with your guardian's consent. If we learn that we have processed personal information of a child under 14 without guardian consent, we will delete it as soon as possible. Guardians can contact us using the details in Section 1 to access, correct or delete such information.
