Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

The AiClothes app is a wardrobe product, and we highly respect your personal information and data. We strive to protect the security and reliability of your personal information and data. This privacy policy is developed and published in accordance with the relevant policies of the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology.

1. Collection of Information: Methods and Scope

  • 1.1 Album and Camera: You can add item and outfit photos from your album or camera. Photos are stored locally on your phone and you can delete them at any time. A compressed item image is uploaded only when you use Smart Recognition; see Section 3.
  • 1.2 Device Information: After you agree to this privacy policy, the app and the third-party SDKs listed below obtain necessary device information, including device model, OS version, network type, IP address, an in-app anonymous identifier (IDFV), and, only if you choose "Allow" in the system tracking prompt, the advertising identifier (IDFA). This is used for usage statistics, service security, and ad delivery and measurement. You can decline tracking, or turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; this does not affect the app's basic features.
  • 1.3 Location: Only when you use the weather-based outfit feature and grant location permission, the app obtains your approximate location (latitude and longitude rounded to two decimal places, about 1 km) to look up local weather. You can decline location access or turn it off at any time in system settings.
  • 1.4 Third-Party SDKs: To provide professional and reliable services, the following SDKs are initialized and run only after you agree to this privacy policy:
    • Umeng+ SDKs (Analytics UMCommon / UMDevice, Performance Monitoring UMAPM, Push UPush): used for app usage statistics, crash and performance analysis, and sending notifications after you allow them. They obtain device model, OS version, network type, IP address, IDFV, IDFA (with your permission) and the push token (Device Token). Provider: Umeng Tongxin (Beijing) Technology Co., Ltd.; privacy policy: https://www.umeng.com/page/policy
    • Pangle (CSJ) Ads SDK with GroMore mediation (including Tencent Ads, Baidu Union and Kuaishou Union ad SDKs): used to show ads and measure ad performance in mainland China. They obtain device model, OS version, network type, IP address, IDFV, IDFA (with your permission), and ad impression and click information. Pangle (CSJ) provider: Beijing Ocean Engine Network Technology Co., Ltd.; privacy policy: https://www.csjplatform.com/privacy
    • Pangle Ads SDK and TikTok Business SDK: used to show ads and measure ad performance outside mainland China (enabled only in overseas regions). They obtain device model, OS version, network type, IP address, IDFA (with your permission), and ad impression and click information. Pangle privacy policy: https://www.pangleglobal.com/privacy
    • QWeather SDK: used to look up weather for weather-based outfit suggestions. It receives the approximate location described above. Privacy policy: https://www.qweather.com/terms/privacy
  • 1.5 Complaints and Reporting: You can tap "Feedback" under "My" in the app to file complaints or report any personal information or other issues you encounter.

2. Information Processing for the Feedback Feature

When you use "Feedback" within the app, we collect and process the following information solely to receive and handle your feedback, communicate with you, and improve our products:

  • 2.1 Information you provide: feedback content, images, and contact information (optional). Contact information is stored encrypted.
  • 2.2 Information attached automatically to help diagnose issues: app version, OS version, device model, language, membership status, and current page, along with an anonymous device identifier (which does not contain your real identity such as name or phone number); your access IP is also recorded at submission solely for abuse prevention and security, and is not retained long-term.
  • 2.3 Storage and third parties: data is stored on Alibaba Cloud (within mainland China, Beijing). Images are uploaded to Alibaba Cloud Object Storage (OSS) and machine-moderated by the Alibaba Cloud DashScope vision model to block non-compliant content; this image moderation is an entrusted-processing arrangement with Alibaba Cloud.
  • 2.4 Retention: images are deleted automatically after 12 months by default; text feedback is retained for 12 months after the ticket is resolved, then cleared automatically.
  • 2.5 Your rights: you can delete all of your feedback (including images) within "My Feedback"; records are removed immediately upon request and images are cleared thereafter.
  • 2.6 Non-public: this channel is a private support ticket between you and the developer; its content is never shown publicly to other users.

3. Information Processing for Smart Recognition

When you use Smart Recognition (automatic recognition when adding items by camera or from your album, and "Re-identify with AI" on the item detail page), we process the following information to automatically identify an item's category, color, season, material and style and to generate a name:

  • 3.1 On-device processing: background removal, initial classification and color extraction are performed by your phone's system capabilities and are not uploaded. Your item photos (including cutouts and original photos) are stored only locally on your phone.
  • 3.2 Information uploaded: to improve accuracy, the app compresses the item image you selected (longest side no more than 768 pixels; when the background can be removed, only the background-removed image is uploaded) and uploads it to our server, together with the app's interface language and an anonymous device identifier generated by the system for this app (which does not contain your real identity such as name or phone number).
  • 3.3 Processors and location: our server is hosted on Alibaba Cloud (within mainland China, Beijing). After receiving the image, our server passes it to Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Qwen vision model) for recognition and returns only the recognition result to the app. This recognition is an entrusted-processing arrangement with Alibaba Cloud, and the image is used solely to complete that recognition.
  • 3.4 Images are not stored: our server does not store the images you upload and does not use them for any purpose other than recognition. Recognition results are stored only locally on your phone.
  • 3.5 Abuse prevention: to limit daily usage and prevent abuse of the service, we count daily requests per anonymous device identifier and per access IP; these counts are kept for at most 2 days and then deleted automatically. Our server access logs record access IP and time as part of routine security operations.
  • 3.6 Your choice: Smart Recognition is optional. You can choose not to use it and add items manually instead; when the network is unavailable, the app uses on-device recognition only and does not upload images.
